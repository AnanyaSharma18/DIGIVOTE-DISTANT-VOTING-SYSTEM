📜 Terms & Conditions

Please read the following terms carefully before proceeding to vote.

🔹 Eligibility to Vote

Only registered voters with valid credentials are permitted to vote. Your details must match the official records for a successful vote.

🔹 Confidentiality & Security

Your voting credentials must remain confidential. Any attempt to share, misuse, or manipulate voting detailswill lead to immediate disqualification.

🔹 One Vote Policy

Each voter is entitled to only one vote. Any attempt at multiple voting will be detected and can result in permanent restriction from the system.

🔹 Irreversible Decision

Once your vote is cast, it cannot be changed or revoked. Please review your choices carefully before confirming your submission.

🔹 Compliance with Laws

Any violation of these terms may be subject to investigation under applicable laws, leading to penalties or legal proceedings.
